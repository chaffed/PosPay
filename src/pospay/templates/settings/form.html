Branding shows throughout the app once you're logged in, and on your own login page (reachable at a slug-based URL) before anyone signs in. Uploading a new logo or favicon replaces the current one; leaving the file field empty keeps whatever's already saved.

Dual control (maker/checker) means a different person must make the final pay/return decision than the one who recommended it — takes effect immediately for every exception, no re-login needed.

Session timeout controls how long a signed-in session lasts before requiring a fresh login (access token) or a full re-authentication (refresh token). Leave a field blank to use the platform default; changes only apply to sessions started after saving.

Data export timeout bounds how long a background export job may run before it's stopped and marked failed — a large organization may need more time than the platform default; leave it blank to use that default.

Auto-import watches a drop directory (issued items, ACH, and check-presentment files your bank's own file transmission can land automatically) and is a single on/off setting for this whole deployment, configured by whoever runs PosPay — not something a tenant can turn on or off itself, which is why it's shown here read-only.

Contact info shows in a footer on every page, including your own login page — leave any field blank to leave it out. The footer doesn't appear at all until at least one field here is filled in.
