An account is one of your bank accounts that PosPay watches for positive-pay matching — every issued item, paid item, stop payment, and ACH activity is tied to one. An account with no customer assigned is a "house" account, visible only to bank-wide staff.

ACH debit mode: filter_by_authorization evaluates debits against your ACH Authorizations; block_all rejects every ACH debit outright, no exceptions.

External account ID (optional) is a customer's own internal reference for the account — once set, bulk file uploads (issued items, paid items, ACH) can use it in place of the real account number, so a customer never has to learn PosPay's own numbering just to submit a file.
