Login, SSO & Account Lockout

PosPay supports password login (with optional passkey step-up) out of the box, plus single sign-on your organization can configure.

{{ docs.screenshot("login", "Login screen") }}

Single sign-on

Configure an SSO connection at the tenant level from Settings → SSO, or at the customer level from a customer's own detail page, if you want a specific customer's users to authenticate through their own identity provider instead of a password.

Account lockout

An account locks automatically after too many failed login attempts, as a brute-force protection. There's no self-service "forgot password" reset — a locked or otherwise stuck account must be unlocked by an administrator from Users. The affected user always receives a lockout email regardless of their own notification preferences, since it's a security event they need to know about — the follow-up unlock notification, once an administrator resolves it, does respect the user's own notification preferences.
