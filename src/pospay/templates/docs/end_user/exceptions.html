Exceptions & Decisioning

The exceptions queue is where checks and ACH items that couldn't be automatically cleared land for review — a check with no matching issued item, or an ACH transaction not covered by an authorization rule. It's a single, network-agnostic queue: check and ACH exceptions are reviewed the same way.

Each exception shows the underlying item's details and, where enough history exists to score it, a machine-learning fraud score with the model version that produced it (items without enough history show "not enough data to score yet" instead of a potentially misleading score).

How to

  • Recommend a decision — enter a proposed pay or return outcome with a reason.
  • Finalize a decision — approve or override the recommendation. If your organization requires dual control, the person who finalizes a decision must be someone other than whoever recommended it — PosPay enforces this automatically and blocks a user from finalizing their own recommendation.
  • Return an ACH exception — you'll be asked to pick a return reason from your organization's managed list of NACHA codes rather than typing free text.

If an exception isn't decided before your organization's response window closes, it may be auto-decided by a default disposition your administrator configured — see ML Scoring & Default Disposition.
